# 🚀 Advanced Booster Ecosystem

The Booster Ecosystem rewards server boosters with custom role management, tiered perks, and buddy sharing.

## 👥 Booster Commands

All booster commands are subcommands of **`/booster`**.

### `/booster role`
Create, customize, or update your personal custom booster role (name, color, icon).
- **Requirements**: Must be an active Server Booster.
- **Usage**: `/booster role <name> <color> [icon]`

### `/booster share`
Share your booster perks with a friend by assigning them as your "Booster Buddy".
- **Requirements**: Requires Tier 2/3 Booster status (based on server configuration).
- **Usage**: `/booster share <user>`

### `/booster status`
View your current booster tier, active perks, and custom role status.
- **Usage**: `/booster status`

## 🛠️ Staff Commands

### `/booster settings`
Configure booster tiers, required boost counts, and allowed perk thresholds.
- **Permission**: `ManageGuild`
