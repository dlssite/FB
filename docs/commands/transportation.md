# 🚀 Advanced Transportation

The Transportation module simulates vehicle ownership, timed geographic journeys, spatial portals, and nation-to-nation transit.

## 🚏 Transit Commands

### `/visit <nation>`
Initiates a journey to another registered Nation or Territory.
- **Mechanic**: Travel time is calculated based on your active vehicle's speed and distance.
- **Prefix**: `$visit <nation>`

### `/teleport <destination>`
Instantly warps to a target location using an active Spatial Rift or Portal token.
- **Prefix**: `$tp <destination>`

### `/transport portal`
Manages, dials, or links active Quantum Portals between territories.
- **Usage**: `/transport portal`

## 🛸 Vehicle Commands

All vehicle maintenance commands are subcommands of **`/vehicle`**.

### `/vehicle status`
Displays your active vehicle's condition, fuel reserves, top speed, and cargo capacity.
- **Usage**: `/vehicle status`

### `/vehicle repair`
Spends repair kits or Embers to restore your vehicle's structural integrity.
- **Usage**: `/vehicle repair`
