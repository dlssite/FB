# 💝 Social Ecosystem

The Social Ecosystem simulates deep interpersonal relationships, allowing players to build friendships, propose marriages, establish family trees, and cultivate Resonance bonds.

## 🤝 Friendship Commands

All friendship commands are subcommands of **`/friend`**.
💡 **Prefix Support**: You can also use `$fadd`, `$flst`, `$frem`, etc. (using your server's prefix).

### `/friend add <user>`
Sends a friendship request to another member.
- **Prefix**: `$fadd <user>`

### `/friend list`
Displays your active friends list and current Resonance levels.
- **Prefix**: `$flst`

### `/friend gift <user> <item>`
Sends a social gift from your inventory to boost your mutual Resonance bond.
- **Prefix**: `$fgft <user> <item>`

### `/friend rival <user>`, `/friend remove <user>`, `/friend profile <user>`
Declare rivalries, dissolve friendships, and view detailed interpersonal compatibility profiles.

## 💍 Marriage Commands

All marriage commands are subcommands of **`/marry`**.

### `/marry propose <user>`
Proposes marriage to your partner. Requires a Wedding Ring or high Resonance bond.
- **Prefix**: `$mpro <user>`

### `/marry profile` & `/marry history`
Displays your shared wedding anniversary, joint Net Worth, active love buffs, and relationship lineage.
- **Prefix**: `$marr`

### `/marry quests`, `/marry vault`, `/marry divorce`
Complete cooperative couple quests, manage your joint bank account, or file for separation.

## 🌳 Family Tree Commands

All family commands are subcommands of **`/family`**.

### `/family found <name>`
Establishes a new Family Dynasty / Bloodline.
- **Prefix**: `$fcre <name>`

### `/family invite <user>`, `/family kick <user>`, `/family claim`
Expand your lineage by adopting members, manage family roles, and claim daily dynastic stipends.
- **Prefix**: `$finv <user>`, `$fkck <user>`

### `/family treasury` & `/family profile`
Access the shared family estate, track collective wealth, and view your expansive genealogical tree.

## ✉️ General Social

### `/social inbox`
Access your personal social notification center to review pending friend requests, marriage proposals, and family invitations.
- **Prefix**: `$inbx`
