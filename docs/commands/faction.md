# 🛡️ Faction System

The Faction System provides a deep, endgame social ecosystem allowing players to establish syndicates, claim headquarters, and wage territorial wars.

## 👥 General Faction Commands

All faction commands are subcommands of **`/faction`**.
💡 **Prefix Support**: You can also use `$faction`, `$raid`, `$vault`, etc. (using your server's prefix).

### `/faction info [faction]`
Displays public intelligence, member roster, active buffs, and HQ status of a Faction.
- **Prefix**: `$faction info`

### `/faction list`
Browses all registered Factions and Syndicates in the server.
- **Prefix**: `$faction list`

### `/faction hq`
Views the internal layout, constructed buildings, and defense grids of your Faction's Headquarters.
- **Prefix**: `$hq`

### `/faction vault`
Accesses the shared Faction Bank to deposit or withdraw Embers and strategic resources.
- **Prefix**: `$vault`
- **Note**: Withdrawals are restricted to Faction Leaders and Quartermasters.

### `/faction hall`
Enters the Faction Hall of Fame to view legendary syndicates and historical monuments.
- **Prefix**: `$hall`

## ⚔️ Military & Diplomatic Commands

### `/faction raid <target>`
Launches a Rivalry Skirmish against an enemy Faction's HQ to plunder Embers.
- **Prefix**: `$raid <target>`

### `/faction expedition`
Deploys a fleet of members on a multi-day deep space expedition for high-tier artifact rewards.
- **Prefix**: `$expedition`

### `/faction campaign`
Declares a persistent Territorial War or Campaign against a rival Faction.
- **Prefix**: `$campaign`

### `/faction diplomacy`
Manages alliances, truces, rivalries, and trade pacts with other Factions.
- **Prefix**: `$diplomacy`

### `/faction board`
Opens the interactive **War Board** to track active bounties, skirmishes, and campaign objectives.
- **Prefix**: `$board`

## 👑 Leadership Commands

### `/faction manage`
Opens the Faction Management Console to adjust roles, mottos, tax rates, and recruit applications.
- **Prefix**: `$faction manage`
- **Requirements**: Faction Leader
