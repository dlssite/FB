# 🎂 Birthday System
Celebrate your special day with the server! This immersive module features automated announcements, a personalized wish wall, and zodiac profiles.

## User Commands
- `/birthday set` — Set your birthday (Day/Month/Year).
- `/birthday check` — View your or another user's birthday profile & zodiac.
- `/birthday list` — See the upcoming 15 birthdays in the server.
- `/birthday wishes` — Read your personal Wish Wall for the current year.
- `/birthday remove` — Delete your birthday data.

## Admin Commands
- `/birthday setting` — Configure the celebration channel, roles, and aesthetic theme.
- `/birthday test` — Trigger a live preview of the birthday card and buttons.

## Celebration Features
- **Announcements**: Automatic rich cards with canvas banners.
- **Wish Wall**: Friends can send interactive wishes via the announcement buttons.
- **Rewards**: Server-funded Bonus Embers are automatically gifted on your birthday.
- **Temporary Roles**: Receive a special 'Birthday' role for exactly 24 hours.
- **Streaks**: Track how many consecutive years you've celebrated with us!
